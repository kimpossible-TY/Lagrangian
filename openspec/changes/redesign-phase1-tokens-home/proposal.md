## Why

The Homepage needs a coherent, accessible visual system while preserving the established Reader and chat appearance.

## What Changes

Add shared additive design tokens and restyle the Homepage using a restrained system-font layout and selective frosted card surfaces. Preserve existing selectors, interactions, and Reader palette values.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `reader-design-tokens`: additive theme roles and Homepage presentation.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

HTML Reader theme and Homepage CSS; no service, JavaScript, or Reader layout changes.

## Non-goals

Reader or chat restyling, new fonts, and changes to account or document behavior.
